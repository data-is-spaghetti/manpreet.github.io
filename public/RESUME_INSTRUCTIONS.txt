PLACE YOUR RESUME PDF HERE
==========================

1. Export your resume as a PDF.
2. Name it exactly:  Manpreet_Kaur_Resume.pdf
3. Drop it into this /public folder.

The "DOWNLOAD RESUME" links in Section 0x00 (Main Engine) and the
2D fallback both point to it automatically.

To use a different filename, change `resumePath` in
src/data/portfolio.js to match.
